My Personal Worldview (Click me)

1. World Relations

In my opinion, war is not a good thing, as it only profits those through whom the country's funds flow. As a believer, I think that no one has the right to take a life, even if it happens to them, they do not have such a right because they did not give life to anyone.

2. Environment

In my opinion, today's environmental situation is extremely critical. Because people excessively use natural resources and have created incredibly cool things for use, but which pollute the environment, we have such poor pollution statistics today.

3. Animals

In my opinion, hunting was relevant when people didn't have stores and were looking for food. Today, I think hunting is a hobby, but it should only be allowed if the hunted animal is used for meat and brings benefit as food on the table. I am against killing for fun. I am also against humans interfering in animal reproduction. Any interference in the wild leads to disaster.

4. Education

In my opinion, learning happens throughout our entire life: we first learn to walk, then to talk, then to draw, and so on. Education is important in the sense that it prevents us from becoming mentally unintelligent. If our brain does not perceive and process information, it will eventually degrade. By the way, when people degrade due to phones, it happens much slower.

5. Spirituality

I am a Christian who has decided to follow the Lord. I attend a Baptist church. Faith is what you do with information. Whether you believe it or not. For example, you may not believe that if you stick your fingers in a socket, you will get shocked. And when you check, it's already an experience. And when you just don't do it because you assume it might happen, you believe this information. Religion is simply a certain direction of what people believe on many issues, which are combined into one science.

6. Government

So, in order: Yes, I have the right to vote in society like others, I have this right because all people are equal in terms of life. Even status or power does not have the right to take away the right to vote. Only one God has power over everyone because He is the one who created us and gave us life. But He also gives people the choice of how to live. In this world, social justice is at a very low level and it has always been so. For me, money is just a resource that helps to survive in the world. It is important in its own way, but this resource can never be the main thing in my life to the point of killing myself to have it. Health care is something that every person should take care of. If we do not take care of ourselves, we are self-destructive, which is a sin before God. Natural resources have been and remain an important resource of the planet. Thanks to civilization, people have learned to create many things artificially, but without natural resources, people cannot survive, so this is an important part of the world's composition.

7. Family

In my opinion, family is what shapes us. It is one of the most important aspects in forming our worldview. As I see from the Bible, family should not always be a priority when you have created your own family, but God highly values family relationships. He even gave a promise recorded in the Bible, Exodus 20 (if I'm not mistaken): "Honor your father and your mother, so that your days may be long in the land." As we see, God promises longevity on earth to those who honor their parents, which indicates the high position of family in a person's life. Friends are no less important in our lives, but in my opinion, they are part of the society that also shapes us.

8. Past/Present/Future

I turn to the Bible and see that God gives each person the choice of how to live on earth. Throughout the Bible, we see that people acted differently and had corresponding consequences. Many people mistakenly believe that God created a certain scenario that everyone lives by, but He simply knows this scenario and does not interfere with it. For example, when people say He is bad because He allowed so much evil in the world, I constantly bring up the argument that many people walk around with unkempt hair not because there is no barber, but because they do not go to one. The same is true with God; people's choice to live without God leads to so much evil in the world. Every decision we make results in a certain outcome, and the decision we make depends on our worldview.
